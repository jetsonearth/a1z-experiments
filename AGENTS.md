# A1Z experiment conventions

- User instruction, 2026-10-02: follow the actual manufacturer SDK and examples for control parameters. Do not introduce custom velocity caps, start-angle gates, torque caps, gravity scaling (such as 0.3), or other speculative restrictions. Keep manufacturer protections intact. Any necessary deviation must have concrete evidence and an explanation to the user.
- User decision, 2026-10-09: the 2026-10-02 forward-dive report is judged a false alarm, and the restrictions added on its basis are removed: the 0.5 rad/s target-rate cap (default is now the SDK's 4 rad/s commanded-velocity saturation), the 20 deg target-gap stop, and the control-test start-gap gate. Do not reintroduce them. The vendor SDK protections, the client heartbeat stop and the --smooth trajectory shaping remain.
- User decision, 2026-10-09: default control for every session (eval, HIL, control test, HIL-SERL rl_run, console) is dimos' gains kp 80/80/80/50/20/20, kd 3/3/3/0.7/0.4/0.4 (robot_proc.DEFAULT_KP/KD), integral ki 2/s capped at 1 deg (on for HIL-SERL too), gripper 2.0 N m while engaged (gripper_max_torque.txt; teach.py accepts 0.5-6.0) and 1.5 N m hold while floating. Gripper heat (fit over 22 sessions, rotor tau ~1.1 min): measured 3.1 N m (setting 4) settles near 97 C, ~2 N m near 60 C; the SDK temperature e-stop (rotor 90 C) covers the six joints only, not the gripper. With the plug aligned, 1.5-2 N m held it without slipping (operator, 2026-10-09). --stiffness now means the legacy vendor-kp x stiffness gains. Evidence: same-demo free-air replay 9.8 mm (vendor) -> 2.8 mm (dimos gains) -> 0.3-0.5 mm (+ integral).
- Prioritize the collect → train → physical evaluation pipeline within the user’s four-hour work session. Do not make full historical trajectory replay, automatic reset, UI polish, or format generalization prerequisites.
- Use this repository as the source of truth. The my-vault experiment directory is a historical copy.
- Preserve raw episodes. Distinguish offline checks, trained checkpoints, and observed physical evaluation.
- The paced CAN transport has observed evidence (joint-6 response loss without pacing); it is a transport fix, not a motion-speed limit.
- Do not send READY or enable motors without the on-site operator’s explicit readiness.
